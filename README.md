Hi, I am [Jie Wang(王杰)](https://everloom-129.github.io/), you can call me Tony. 
- 🎓 I just earned my M.S. in Robotics from the [GRASP Lab](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) at the University of Pennsylvania. Previously I received a dual BS in Computer Engineering at ZJU and UIUC.
- 🦾 My thesis, *Evaluating, Enhancing, and Explaining Robot Foundation Models*, was done in the [GRASP Lab](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) under Prof. Dinesh Jayaraman and Prof. Kostas Daniilidis.
- ⭐️ My interests focus on VLAs and robot learning — especially the evaluation, benchmarking, and interpretability of robot foundation models.
- 💼 Previously I interned at DYNA-Robotics, constructing agentic reasoning for general purpose VLA models, and at IDEA Shenzhen's `Computer Vision & Robotics` Department, I worked on VLMs for video captioning.
- 🌱 I'm diving deeper into advanced topics like Multimodal LLM + Robotics.
